The short version

This app collects nothing. It has no servers, and the developer never sees your data.

What the app stores

Everything you type — dates, times, notes, tags and settings — is stored on your iPhone. If you have iCloud enabled it is also stored in your own private iCloud (CloudKit) database, tied to your Apple ID. Only you can read it. The developer has no access to it, and neither does anyone else.

What the app sends

Nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code of any kind. The app makes no network requests other than Apple's own iCloud synchronisation, which happens between your device and your own iCloud account, and the App Store purchase check.

If the app crashes

If you have turned on Apple's own “Share With App Developers” setting, Apple may send the developer a report when an app crashes. Those reports come from Apple, never from this app, and they contain no part of your log — no notes, no names, no dates, nothing you have typed. They describe the reason for the crash, so it can be fixed. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

What the app can access on your device

Nothing beyond its own storage. The app does not access your contacts, your location, your camera, your microphone, your photos, your calendar or your health data.

Files you create

The app writes automatic backup files into its own private storage. They are not browsable in the Files app or from a computer, and they are encrypted while your device is locked. You restore from them inside the app. Exported JSON, CSV and PDF files are different: once you share or move one it is outside the app, and you control where it goes.

Information about other people

You may record information about other people, such as the other parent or your child. That information is yours, stays on your device and in your own iCloud, and is never transmitted to the developer or any third party. You are responsible for how you use and share anything you export.

Children

The app is intended for use by adults. It is not directed at children and collects no information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its local data. To remove the synced copy, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your device.

Legal note

This app is a record-keeping tool. It does not provide legal advice, and nothing it produces is guaranteed to be admissible in any legal proceedings.

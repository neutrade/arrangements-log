Arrangements Log

A private record of child contact arrangements, for iPhone.

Arrangements Log helps you keep your own factual, dated record of the calls and visits in a child contact arrangement: whether each one happened, how the actual times compared with the scheduled ones, what issues arose, and how your child seemed afterwards.

Private by design. Everything stays on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud. There are no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and the developer cannot see your data.

A record-keeping tool, not legal advice

Whether anything you record is useful or admissible in legal proceedings is a matter for your solicitor and the court. The app makes no promises about either.
